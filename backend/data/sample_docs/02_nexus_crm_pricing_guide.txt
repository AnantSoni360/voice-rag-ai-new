DETAILED PLAN DESCRIPTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STARTER PLAN — ₹999 per user per month
Designed for small teams and solopreneurs starting with CRM.
- Up to 5,000 contacts
- 1 sales pipeline
- Basic lead management and task tracking
- Standard email templates (10 templates included)
- Mobile app access
- Community support and knowledge base
- 5 GB document storage
- GST-compliant invoicing summary

Annual billing: ₹799 per user per month (save 20%)

PROFESSIONAL PLAN — ₹2,499 per user per month
Our most popular plan for growing sales teams.
- Unlimited contacts and companies
- 5 sales pipelines with automation rules
- Advanced email sequences and WhatsApp integration
- Custom reports and dashboards
- REST API and webhook access
- Tally and Zoho Books integration
- 50 GB storage per organization
- Priority support (12-hour response SLA)
- Onboarding session included (2 hours with a Nexus expert)
- Hindi and English UI

Annual billing: ₹1,999 per user per month (save 20%)
Minimum 3 users on Professional.

ENTERPRISE PLAN — Custom Pricing (minimum 20 users)
Built for large organizations needing maximum control and customization.
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- Custom deployment options (cloud or on-premise)
- Unlimited pipelines, templates, and storage
- Advanced AI-powered sales forecasting
- Centralized admin console and SSO (SAML 2.0, Google, Microsoft)
- Role-based access control with custom permission sets
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager
- 2-hour support SLA with 24/7 emergency escalation
- Custom contract and purchase order support
- Data residency options (Mumbai, Hyderabad, or your own datacenter)
- Quarterly business review meetings
- White-labeling available for resellers

Enterprise pricing is determined after a consultation call. Typical range: ₹4,000–₹8,000 per user per month depending on user count and customizations.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
