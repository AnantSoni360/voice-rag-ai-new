NEXUS CRM — ONBOARDING GUIDE
Getting Started in 5 Steps | October 2024

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1: ACCOUNT SETUP (Day 1)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After signing up:
1. Log in at app.nexuscrm.in
2. Complete your organization profile (company name, industry, GST number)
3. Upload your company logo
4. Set your time zone and date format (IST is default)
5. Enable two-factor authentication for your admin account

Select your language: Go to Settings > Language > Hindi or English.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2: INVITE YOUR TEAM (Day 1–2)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Go to Settings > Users > Invite Users.
- Enter email addresses (bulk invite via CSV supported)
- Assign roles: Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Representative, or Viewer
- Users receive an invitation email and can set up in 2 minutes

Tip: Start with 3–5 power users before rolling out to the whole team.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3: IMPORT YOUR DATA (Day 2–3)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Go to Settings > Import > Contacts or Deals.
- Download the Nexus CRM import template (CSV)
- Map your existing columns to Nexus CRM fields
- Upload and review for errors
- Confirm import

Data migration from Salesforce, Zoho, HubSpot, or Freshsales:
Contact support@nexuscrm.in — migration assistance is free for Professional
and Enterprise customers.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4: SET UP YOUR PIPELINE (Day 3–4)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Go to Sales > Pipelines > Create Pipeline.
Recommended default stages for a B2B sales pipeline:
1. New Lead
2. Qualified
3. Demo Scheduled
4. Proposal Sent
5. Negotiation
6. Closed Won / Closed Lost

Customize stage names and add automation rules (e.g., auto-assign a task when
a deal moves to "Proposal Sent").

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5: SET UP INTEGRATIONS (Day 4–5)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Go to Settings > Integrations.
Recommended first integrations:
- Gmail or Outlook (sync emails automatically)
- WhatsApp Business (automated follow-ups)
- Google Calendar (schedule demo calls from within Nexus CRM)
- Tally (invoice sync)

All integrations include a setup wizard with step-by-step instructions.
