NEXUS CRM — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
Updated: October 2024

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GENERAL QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What is Nexus CRM?
A: Nexus CRM is a cloud-based CRM and project management platform designed for Indian
businesses. It helps teams manage leads, track deals, automate follow-ups, and
collaborate efficiently — in Hindi and English.

Q: Is Nexus CRM suitable for my industry?
A: Nexus CRM is used across IT services, real estate, education, manufacturing,
financial services, healthcare, and e-commerce. If your business involves sales,
customer follow-ups, or team coordination, Nexus CRM is a strong fit.

Q: Does Nexus CRM support Hindi?
A: Yes. The entire Nexus CRM interface, reports, and mobile apps are available in Hindi.
You can switch languages in the settings at any time.

Q: How long does it take to set up?
A: Most teams are up and running within 1–3 days. The Professional plan includes a
2-hour onboarding session with a Nexus expert to accelerate setup.

Q: Can I import data from my existing CRM or Excel?
A: Yes. Nexus CRM supports CSV and Excel import for contacts, leads, and deals.
Dedicated migration support is available for imports from Salesforce, Zoho, HubSpot,
and Freshsales at no additional cost for Professional and Enterprise customers.

PRICING AND BILLING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What is the price of Nexus CRM?
A: Starter: ₹999/user/month. Professional: ₹2,499/user/month. Enterprise: custom quote.
Annual billing saves 20% on all plans.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes. All plans offer a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start.

Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
A: Yes. You can upgrade at any time and the new charges will be prorated.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

Q: Do you offer discounts for NGOs or startups?
A: Yes. Registered NGOs receive a 30% discount. Startups under 2 years old with fewer
than 20 employees qualify for our Startup Program at 50% off Starter for 12 months.
Contact sales@nexuscrm.in to apply.

Q: Is GST included in the prices?
A: All listed prices are exclusive of GST. GST at 18% is applicable and shown separately
on your invoice.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: UPI, NEFT/RTGS, credit card, debit card, net banking, and corporate cheque.
Purchase order support is available for Enterprise customers.

FEATURES AND INTEGRATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Does Nexus CRM integrate with WhatsApp?
A: Yes, on Professional and Enterprise plans. WhatsApp Business API integration requires
a one-time activation fee of ₹2,999. It enables automated WhatsApp follow-up sequences.

Q: Can I integrate Nexus CRM with Tally?
A: Yes. Nexus CRM integrates with Tally ERP 9 and Tally Prime for invoice syncing
and payment tracking. Available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

Q: Does Nexus CRM have a mobile app?
A: Yes. The Nexus CRM app is available on Android (Play Store) and iOS (App Store).
Offline mode is supported with sync when you reconnect.

Q: How many users can I add?
A: Starter supports 1 to unlimited users (billed per user). Professional requires
minimum 3 users. Enterprise requires minimum 20 users.

Q: Does Nexus CRM support role-based access control?
A: Basic roles (Admin, Manager, Sales Rep) are available on all plans.
Custom role-based access control (RBAC) with granular permission sets is available
on the Enterprise plan.

SUPPORT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What support options are available?
A: All plans get access to our knowledge base and community forum.
Starter: email support with 48-hour response SLA.
Professional: priority email and chat support with 12-hour SLA.
Enterprise: 24/7 phone, email, and chat with 2-hour SLA plus a dedicated CSM.

Q: Do you offer training?

Professional plan includes a 2-hour live onboarding session.
Paid team training sessions (up to 10 participants) are available at ₹4,999 per session.

Q: In what languages is support available?
A: Support is available in English and Hindi.

SECURITY AND DATA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: All data is stored in India — our data centers are located in Mumbai and Hyderabad.
Enterprise customers may request custom data residency.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes. Nexus CRM uses AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
We conduct annual third-party security audits and are SOC 2 Type II compliant.

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. You can export all your contacts, deals, tasks, and reports to CSV or Excel
at any time. A full data export for account closure is available within 30 days
of subscription cancellation at no charge.

Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?
A: Your data is retained for 60 days after cancellation, during which you can export
it. After 60 days, data is permanently deleted from our servers.
