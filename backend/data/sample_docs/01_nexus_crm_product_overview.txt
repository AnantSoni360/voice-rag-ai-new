WHAT IS NEXUS CRM?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Nexus CRM is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management and Project Management
platform designed for Indian SMEs and enterprises. It helps sales teams track leads,
manage customer interactions, automate follow-ups, and close deals faster.

Nexus CRM is available in English and Hindi, with a mobile app for Android and iOS.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CORE MODULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. SALES PIPELINE MANAGEMENT
   - Visual Kanban board for deal tracking
   - Customizable pipeline stages
   - Automated deal progression alerts
   - Win/loss analysis reports

2. CONTACT & LEAD MANAGEMENT
   - Unlimited contacts and companies
   - Lead capture from web forms, email, and phone
   - Duplicate detection and merge
   - 360-degree customer view with interaction history

3. TASK & PROJECT MANAGEMENT
   - Assign tasks to team members
   - Set deadlines and priority levels
   - Gantt chart view for project timelines
   - Dependency tracking

4. EMAIL & COMMUNICATION AUTOMATION
   - Built-in email sequences and templates
   - WhatsApp Business integration
   - Automated follow-up reminders
   - Email open and click tracking

5. REPORTING & ANALYTICS
   - Real-time dashboards
   - Custom report builder
   - Sales forecasting
   - Team performance metrics

6. INTEGRATION HUB
   - 50+ pre-built integrations
   - REST API and webhooks
   - Zapier and Make (Integromat) connectors
   - Tally and Zoho Books integration for invoicing

WHO IS NEXUS CRM FOR?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Nexus CRM is best suited for:
- B2B sales teams of 5 to 1000+ users
- Companies with a structured sales process
- Businesses needing multi-language support (Hindi + English)
- Organizations looking to replace spreadsheets with a proper CRM

Typical customers include IT services companies, real estate agencies, manufacturing
firms, financial services, and edtech companies across India.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DATA STORAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

All customer data is stored in data centers located in Mumbai and Hyderabad, India.
Nexus CRM is compliant with India's PDPB (Personal Data Protection Bill) guidelines.
Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
