NEXUS CRM — INTEGRATION GUIDE
Supported Integrations | October 2024

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMUNICATION INTEGRATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GMAIL AND GOOGLE WORKSPACE
Available on: All plans
- Two-way email sync
- Automatic contact creation from email signatures
- Email tracking (open and click notifications)
- Send emails from within Nexus CRM using your Gmail account

MICROSOFT 365 (OUTLOOK)
Available on: Professional and Enterprise
- Outlook email sync
- Teams meeting scheduling from Nexus CRM
- SharePoint document linking

WHATSAPP BUSINESS API
Available on: Professional and Enterprise (₹2,999 one-time setup)
- Send automated WhatsApp messages in sequences
- Templates must be pre-approved by WhatsApp
- Inbound WhatsApp messages logged in contact timeline
- Supported languages: Hindi and English

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ACCOUNTING AND BILLING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TALLY PRIME AND TALLY ERP 9
Available on: Professional and Enterprise
- Sync invoices and payments from Tally to Nexus CRM
- Create quotations in Nexus CRM that push to Tally
- View payment status against deals

ZOHO BOOKS
Available on: Professional and Enterprise
- Bidirectional sync of invoices, payments, and contacts
- GST report alignment between Nexus CRM and Zoho Books

RAZORPAY
Available on: Professional and Enterprise
- Track payments received against deals
- Payment link generation from within Nexus CRM
- Automated deal closure on payment confirmation

CALENDAR AND SCHEDULING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GOOGLE CALENDAR
Available on: All plans
- Sync Nexus CRM tasks and meetings with Google Calendar
- Schedule demo calls with one click using Calendly-style booking links
- Meeting reminders pushed to calendar

MICROSOFT OUTLOOK CALENDAR
Available on: Professional and Enterprise

AUTOMATION PLATFORMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ZAPIER
Available on: Professional and Enterprise
- Connect Nexus CRM with 5,000+ apps via Zapier
- Trigger Zaps when a deal is created, updated, or moved

MAKE (INTEGROMAT)
Available on: Professional and Enterprise
- Visual automation builder for complex workflows

NEXUS CRM REST API
Available on: Professional and Enterprise
- Full REST API with OAuth 2.0 authentication
- Webhooks for real-time event notifications
- API rate limit: 1,000 requests/minute (Professional), 10,000/minute (Enterprise)
- API documentation: developers.nexuscrm.in
