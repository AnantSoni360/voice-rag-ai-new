CASE STUDY 1: BRIGHTPATH EDTECH
Industry: Education Technology | Plan: Professional | Users: 28
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Challenge:
BrightPath EdTech, a Pune-based online learning platform, was managing 3,000+
parent leads in a shared Excel sheet. Sales reps were missing follow-up calls,
leads were falling through the cracks, and management had no visibility into
the pipeline.

Solution:
BrightPath moved to Nexus CRM Professional in January 2024. They set up a 6-stage
admissions pipeline, configured WhatsApp automated follow-ups in Hindi and English,
and integrated with their course management system via API.

Results (6 months):
- Lead conversion rate: 12% → 31%
- Average follow-up time: 3 days → 4 hours (automated WhatsApp sequence)
- Sales team productivity: +45% (less time on manual tracking)
- Monthly revenue increase: ₹18 lakhs attributable to improved lead handling

Testimonial:
"Nexus CRM changed the way we handle admissions. Our counsellors spend their time
actually talking to parents instead of updating spreadsheets." — Priya Menon, VP Sales

CASE STUDY 2: DELTA INFRASTRUCTURE PVT. LTD.
Industry: Real Estate | Plan: Enterprise | Users: 120
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Challenge:
Delta Infrastructure manages real estate projects across Mumbai and Pune. With 120
sales staff, 4 project sites, and thousands of property leads, they needed a system
that could handle complex workflows, multi-city teams, and Tally integration.

Solution:
Nexus CRM Enterprise with custom deployment on a dedicated cloud instance.
Territory-based access control, Tally Prime integration for booking payments,
and custom pipeline stages for each project phase.

Results (12 months):
- Centralized pipeline across 4 sites — no more siloed spreadsheets
- Payment tracking against deals reduced disputes by 60%
- Manager reporting time: 3 hours/week → 20 minutes/week (automated dashboards)
- Site visit scheduling automated via Google Calendar integration

Testimonial:
"Before Nexus CRM, we had four Excel files, four teams, and zero coordination.
Now we have one source of truth." — Rajesh Nair, Director of Sales

CASE STUDY 3: FINSERV PARTNERS
Industry: Financial Services / Insurance | Plan: Professional | Users: 45
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Challenge:
FinServ Partners, a Mumbai-based financial advisory firm, needed a compliant CRM that
could handle sensitive client data, support Hindi-speaking advisors, and integrate with
their document management workflow.

Solution:
Nexus CRM Professional with MFA enabled, Hindi UI for field advisors, and API
integration with their document vault. Custom fields for policy numbers, renewal dates,
and premium amounts.

Results:
- Policy renewal reminders automated: 0 missed renewals in 6 months
- New advisors onboarded 60% faster with the Hindi UI
- Client satisfaction score (NPS): 42 → 67 within 8 months
- Audit compliance: full client interaction history available for regulatory review

Testimonial:
"The Hindi interface was the deciding factor for us. Our field advisors are far more
comfortable and productive." — Anita Kapoor, Head of Operations
