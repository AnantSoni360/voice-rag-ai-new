NEXUS CRM — SALES OBJECTION HANDLING PLAYBOOK
For Nexus Sales Representatives | Confidential | October 2024

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
INTRODUCTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This playbook provides approved responses to the most common customer objections.
Do not make promises beyond what is documented here. If a customer requests a
discount or exception not covered in this playbook, escalate to your sales manager.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTION 1: "IT'S TOO EXPENSIVE"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Customer says: "₹2,499 per user is too much for us."

Approved responses:

Response A — ROI framing:
"I understand price is a consideration. Most of our Professional customers find that
Nexus CRM recovers its cost within 60–90 days through time savings on manual tracking
and faster deal closures. For a 10-person team, the monthly cost is ₹24,990 — but if
your team closes even one additional deal per month, the ROI is very clear."

Response B — Annual billing:
"Our annual billing option brings Professional down to ₹1,999 per user per month —
a 20% saving. For a 10-user team, that's a saving of ₹60,000 over a year."

Response C — Starter as entry point:
"If budget is a concern right now, our Starter plan at ₹999 per user per month gives
your team the core CRM features. You can upgrade to Professional when you're ready."

Response D — Free trial:
"The best way to see the value is to use it. Our 14-day free trial requires no credit
card. Let's get your team started and we can revisit pricing once you've seen the impact."

What NOT to say:
- Do not offer discounts beyond the published volume or annual discounts without manager approval.
- Do not promise custom pricing to teams with fewer than 50 users without escalation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTION 2: "WE'RE ALREADY USING EXCEL / SPREADSHEETS"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Customer says: "We manage everything in Excel. Why do we need a CRM?"

Approved response:
"Excel works well for small data sets, but as your team grows, it creates challenges:
version conflicts when multiple people edit, no automated follow-up reminders, no
visibility into who did what, and no forecasting. Nexus CRM gives you a single source
of truth, automated workflows, and real-time dashboards — so your managers can see
pipeline health without asking each rep for an update. We also import your existing
Excel data for free, so migration is seamless."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTION 3: "WE'RE NOT READY TO SWITCH RIGHT NOW"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Customer says: "This looks good, but now is not the right time."

Approved responses:

Response A — Timing urgency:
"I completely understand timing matters. May I ask — is there a specific event (quarter
end, team expansion, financial year start) after which it would be a better time?
I can schedule a follow-up then so you don't have to start from scratch."

Response B — Free trial offer:
"Starting a 14-day trial requires zero commitment and no credit card. You can explore
the platform at your own pace, and we can pick up the conversation whenever you're ready."

Response C — Cost of delay:
"Every month without a proper CRM is a month where leads may fall through the cracks.
The free trial lets you start capturing leads today without disrupting your current process."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTION 4: "WE TRIED A CRM BEFORE AND IT DIDN'T WORK"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Customer says: "We tried Salesforce / Zoho before and our team didn't adopt it."

Approved response:
"That's a very common experience, and it's usually not about the product — it's about
the implementation. The biggest reasons CRM implementations fail are: lack of proper
onboarding, too complex a system for the team's needs, and no internal champion to
drive adoption. Nexus CRM is designed to be simpler than Salesforce or Zoho for Indian
teams. Our Professional plan includes a 2-hour onboarding session, and our UI is
available in Hindi to lower the learning curve. We'd love to show you a demo tailored
to your team's workflow."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTION 5: "WE NEED TO THINK ABOUT IT / DISCUSS WITH THE TEAM"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Customer says: "I need to discuss this with my manager / co-founder / team."

Approved response:
"Absolutely — this is a team decision and it makes sense to involve everyone. Would
it be helpful if I sent you a one-page summary of Nexus CRM's features and pricing
that you can share internally? I can also arrange a 30-minute demo call with your
full team at a time that works. Who else would typically be involved in this decision?"

Follow-up: Try to identify the decision-maker and schedule a group demo.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OBJECTION 6: "DO YOU HAVE A FEATURE FOR [SPECIFIC NEED]?"
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

If the feature exists: Confirm from the product documentation and offer a demo.
If the feature doesn't exist: "That's a great requirement. This isn't in our current
release, but I'll log it as a feature request. Our product team reviews requests
quarterly. In the meantime, many customers solve this using our webhook/API integration
with [relevant tool]."

Do not promise a feature that does not exist or a specific delivery date.
