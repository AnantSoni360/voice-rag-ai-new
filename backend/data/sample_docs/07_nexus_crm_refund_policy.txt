NEXUS CRM — REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY
Effective: January 2024

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CANCELLATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You may cancel your Nexus CRM subscription at any time from the Account Settings page
or by contacting support@nexuscrm.in.

Monthly subscriptions: Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.
Annual subscriptions: Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current annual term.

After cancellation, your account enters a 60-day grace period during which you can:
- Export all your data in CSV or Excel format
- Re-activate your subscription at the same plan and price

After 60 days, all data is permanently and irreversibly deleted.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
REFUND POLICY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Monthly plans:
- No refunds are issued for the current billing month after payment is processed.
- If you cancel before the next billing date, no further charges apply.

Annual plans:
- Full refund within 14 days of the start of a new annual subscription, if the
  product does not meet the features as described at the time of purchase.
- Pro-rata refund for the unused months if cancellation is requested within 30 days
  of annual renewal and the organization has fewer than 50 users.
- No refund for cancellations requested after 30 days of annual renewal.

Free trial period:
- No charges are made during the 14-day free trial.
- If you do not enter billing details, the account is automatically deactivated
  after the trial without any charge.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
EXCEPTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Refunds may be considered in exceptional circumstances (e.g., extended downtime
caused by Nexus infrastructure failure beyond the SLA limit). Such requests are
reviewed by the Finance team within 10 business days.

SLA credits (not refunds) are issued when uptime falls below the guaranteed 99.9%
in any calendar month. Credit amount: 10% of monthly fee per 1 hour of excess downtime,
capped at one month's fee.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Email: billing@nexuscrm.in
Subject line: Refund Request — [Your Organization Name] — [Invoice Number]
Include: Reason for refund, invoice number, and bank details for NEFT transfer.
Processing time: 7–10 business days after approval.
